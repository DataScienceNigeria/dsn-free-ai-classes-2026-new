
# Submission Guide 📋

Congratulations on completing your learning track! 🎉  
Follow the steps below to submit your proof of completion and receive your DSN Certificate.

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step Submission Process

### **Step 1: Complete Your Course**
- Finish all modules and required assessments  
- Ensure completion is within your **7-day cohort window**

---

### **Step 2: Capture Proof of Completion**
You can submit any of the following:
- Screenshot of completion badge  
- Course certificate  
- Dashboard showing 100% progress  

> ⚠️ Ensure your **name and course details are visible**

---

### **Step 3: Fill the Submission Form**
- Use the official submission link shared via: https://forms.gle/DWEjYCtMB2ASSjga9---

### **Step 4: Verification**
- Submissions will be reviewed by the DSN team  
- Only valid and complete submissions will be approved  

---

### **Step 5: Receive Your Certificate**
- Your **DSN Certificate of Completion** will be sent via email  
- Top performers will be contacted separately for **Microsoft Vouchers**

---

## 🏆 Tips to Stand Out

- Complete your course early  
- Engage fully with all modules  
- Ensure your submission is clear and valid  

---

## ❗ Important Notes

- Late submissions may not be accepted  
- Incomplete or unclear proof will be rejected  
- Each participant can submit only once per cohort
