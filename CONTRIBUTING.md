# Contributing to DSN Free AI Classes 2026 🤝

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **DSN Free AI Classes 2026** initiative.

We welcome contributions from community members, mentors, and partners to improve learning resources, documentation, and overall participant experience.

---

## 📌 How You Can Contribute

### 1. Improve Documentation
- Fix typos or unclear instructions  
- Enhance onboarding guides  
- Add examples or clarifications  

### 2. Add Learning Resources
- Share useful tutorials, tools, or references  
- Suggest additional datasets or practice materials  

### 3. Support Participants
- Answer questions  
- Share insights and best practices  
- Mentor beginners in the community
